Emu Call Check

A call is confirmed when at least two of its reads match one of its references almost exactly. An unconfirmed call has no such reads: on the run used to evaluate the check, these were calls made from chimeric reads, but a species far from every reference looks the same. Confirmed calls can still be contaminants, so compare with the negative controls. Nothing is removed from the emu results. Full table: emu_call_check.tsv.
